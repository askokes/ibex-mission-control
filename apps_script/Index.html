The one goal

Marketing ROI

Weighted marketing-generated revenue divided by marketing spend. Every objective and task below exists to move this number. If it doesn’t feed ROI, it doesn’t make the plan.

Purpose statement (draft for Julie): Marketing exists to grow ibex’s revenue. We make ibex the name buyers find, trust and choose, and we prove it in pipeline.

Target: set with Julie in the 2027 plan. Tracking starts with the Dec 15 site launch. FY27 marketing budget: about $2.48M.
